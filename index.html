How it works

  1. Paid discovery sprint

    One or two weeks. You get a clickable prototype or tech plan and a fixed quote. The fee counts toward the build.

  2. Weekly demos

    Progress on a live staging link every week, so there are no surprises at the end.

  3. Milestone billing

    40% to start, 30% at the main milestone, 30% at launch.

  4. Clean handover

    Code lives in your GitHub from day one. You own everything once each milestone is paid.
